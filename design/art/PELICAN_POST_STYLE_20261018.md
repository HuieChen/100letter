## Match the drawing language

Use the actual inset gameplay artwork: dark plum/near-black chunky contours; crisp pixel-stepped silhouettes; rounded, slightly exaggerated geometry; large flat color planes; at most one local shadow tone; sparse internal marks. Warm cream, teal, coral, dusty blue and muted plum fit Solmere. Maintain strong separation of figures and scenery. Avoid dry pastel noise, blanket distressed texture, glossy lighting and a detailed rendered-painting appearance.

The postcard frame, oversized envelopes and black surround in the supplied official marketing images are not the gameplay composition. Do not paste those around our scenes. Do not copy Pepper, Beatrice, names, logos, text or exact architecture. Create original Dear Somewhere characters and places in the same visual language.

## Preserve gameplay geometry

Every generated scene has a layout contract based on the existing AI-generated scene: door, notice, mail slot, removable plaque and walking floor stay at their actual interaction coordinates. Empty ground remains traversable. No new foreground plant or attractive door may be added as decoration without a complete interaction. Inspect every candidate before changing runtime selection.

Paper remains one object, with runtime writing inside the real sheet. No baked text in generated blank paper. Props require clean exterior alpha, visible silhouettes, no white fringe and useful blank writing area. A magnifier's lens is transparent, not a painted white disk. Atlas regions must be measured from actual output, never copied blindly from a former sheet.
